_______SAFETY LAWS AND RIGHT FOR WOMEN_______

RIGHT TO EQUALITY

Women have the same legal rights as men . Discrimination based on gender is prohibited

RIGHT TO EDUCATION

Every women and girl has the right to receive education

RIGHT TO EQUAL PAY

women should receive equal pay for equal work

RIGHT AGAINST DOMESTIC VIOLENCE

women are protected from physical, emotional, verbal, and economic abuse under the protection of women from domestic violence act,2005.

RIGHT TO PROTECTION FROM SEXUAL HARASSMENT

women are protected from sexual harassment at workplaces under the sexual harassment of women at workplace act,2013.

DOWRY PROHIBITION Act,1961

Makes giving, taking or demanding dowry illegal.

CRIMINAL LAW ACT,2013

Strengthened laws related to stalking,acid attacts,sexual assault,and harassment.

RIGHT TO MATERNITY BENEFITS

Working women are entitled to maternity leave and related benefits under the maternity benefit act,1961.
