2.Avoid walking along in isolated areas at night.

3.Keep your phone charged.

4.Save Emergency contacts.

5.Trust your instincts.

6.Call for help if you feel unsafe.

_______ KEY VULNERABLE TARGETS_______

EYES

Eyes gouging or striking impairs vision and causes intense pain.

NOSE

Hitting this area causes disorientation and watering eyes.

THROAT

Striking the throat restricts breathing and causes an attacker to gag.

GROIN

A highly sensitive area that can incapacitate an attacker instantly.

_______ Essential Self-Defense Move _______

THE PALM STRIKE

How: Keep your hand open and pull your fingers slightly back. Drive the stiff, fleshy heel of your palm directly upward into the attacker’s nose or chin.

Why: It causes immediate disorientation and drives the head back.

THE GROIN KICK

How: Plant your supporting foot and drive the shoelaces or knee of your dominant leg upward into the groin.

Why: It causes immense pain and forces the attacker to bend forward, bringing their head into range for a follow-up strike.

THE ELBOW STRIKE

How: Bend your arm, plant your feet, and drive your elbow forcefully into the attacker’s jaw, temple, or ribs, using your core and hip rotation for power.

Why: The elbow is a hard, bony surface that can deliver massive kinetic energy in close quarters.

WRIST ESCAPE

How: Rotate your arm so that the thumb of your grabbed hand faces toward the gap between the attacker's thumb and index finger. Pull your arm forcefully back toward your own body through that gap

Why: This leverages your movement against the weakest part of their hand grip.


_______ CORE PRINCIPLES _______

USE SURPRISE

The element of surprise is your greatest asset. Use sudden yelling or unexpected movements to throw the attacker off balance mentally

YELL LOUDLY

Yelling "Fire" or shouting "No!" firmly can break the role of a victim, distract the attacker, and alert bystanders to your situation.

RUN FIRST

Self-defense is ultimately about escaping danger, not winning a fight. The moment you create distance or the attacker loosens their grip, run toward a populated area or safe location.
